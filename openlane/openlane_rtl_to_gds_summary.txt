1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY
--------------------
- The metrics CSV reports flow_status = "flow completed". Total runtime is 0h2m17s; routed runtime is 0h1m56s; peak memory is 549.52 MB.
- Target platform is SkyWater SKY130A using sky130_fd_sc_hd, with a 10 ns (100 MHz) clock constraint and target floorplan utilization of 45%.
- Post-synthesis statistics report 840 cells in trng_top and 841 cells in the expanded design hierarchy. The detailed area report also says some custom wrapper cell areas are unknown, so its chip-area number must be treated cautiously.

- Magic DRC reports COUNT: 0 and LVS reports zero errors. Several router violation counters are zero. However, KLayout DRC and CVC metrics are -1 (not reported/available), despite being enabled in the rerun configuration; they must not be stated as passing.
- The ZIP does not contain a final GDS file. It contains reports, a post-synthesis gate-level netlist, RTL, a tech-cell file, configurations, and timing constraints.

8. IMPORTANT MISSING / CAUTION ITEMS FOR A PAPER
------------------------------------------------
- Final GDS and final DEF are not in the ZIP, although the metrics indicate flow completion.
- KLayout DRC and CVC are not verifiable from the included reports (-1 metrics); do not claim these passed.
- Standard-cell area for the custom RO wrappers/sampler is reported as unknown. Area comparisons should be qualified until the cell mapping/area accounting is confirmed.
- Metrics CSV has inconsistent cell-count fields (synth_cell_count=2 and cells_pre_abc=2 versus detailed 840-cell mapped report), and Final_Util=-1. Use source report-specific values, and flag the CSV anomalies.
- One unconstrained endpoint and four max-fanout violations are reported by the timing-check report, in addition to eight combinational loops corresponding to the ring oscillator bank.
- Verilator build failed due to a missing g++ binary path; width warnings should be reviewed and the simulation rerun in a working compiler environment.
- The reported 100 MHz is the constrained clock, not an experimentally validated maximum frequency.
