The FPGA implementation of the ring-oscillator-based true random number generator was evaluated using Vivado power estimation and static timing analysis. The estimated total on-chip power was 73 mW, comprising 1 mW of dynamic power and 72 mW of device static power. The reported junction temperature was 25.4 °C under a typical process corner, with a medium confidence level for the power estimate. Static timing analysis reported a worst setup slack of +2.858 ns, worst hold slack of +0.106 ns, and worst pulse-width slack of +4.500 ns. No setup, hold, or pulse-width failing endpoints were reported, and Vivado indicated that all user-specified timing constraints were met.
